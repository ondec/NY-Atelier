# NY Atelier — UX reference notes

These references informed the **interaction patterns**, not the assets, layouts, trademarks or code copied from another brand. Keep NY Atelier's own editorial identity and imagery in the final design.

- Luxury editorial sections: [FARFETCH](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ba7d9306-7103-4f8d-bc9e-6b3048f5121e) and [SSENSE](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4ff94654-4570-46c2-beaf-77b5ccaf97ce) on Mobbin.
- Product information density and size selection: [ZARA product screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/364bc07c-1238-43f7-bd0c-ac130dbc6f75) on Mobbin.
- Shopping-to-checkout journey inspiration: [H&M checkout flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/d19485c3-1be5-44b2-bd54-5b3e03818c06) on Mobbin.

## NY Atelier-specific choices

- Five top-level pages: Home; RTW Store; Services; Our Story; Contact.
- Collections lives under RTW Store, not in the main navigation.
- Consultations live under Contact, not in the main navigation.
- Primary emphasis: editorial fashion photography, spacious layout, clear GHS prices, simple product selection, and coherent mobile navigation.
- This version has a demo-only checkout; it does not communicate with real payments, bookings or inventory.
