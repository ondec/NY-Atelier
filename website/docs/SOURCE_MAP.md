## Mobbin benchmark — UX patterns only

### Editorial website sections
- FARFETCH section: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ba7d9306-7103-4f8d-bc9e-6b3048f5121e
- FARFETCH section: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/cdff4ac0-1d2a-46c6-b6ca-de21f268484c
- SSENSE section: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/4ff94654-4570-46c2-beaf-77b5ccaf97ce

Design principles selected for NY: confident large-format editorial photography, clear section rhythm, room for whitespace, and navigation that stays secondary to the collection story.

### Product discovery & detail
- ZARA web product detail: https://mobbin.com/screens/364bc07c-1238-43f7-bd0c-ac130dbc6f75
- H&M web product detail: https://mobbin.com/screens/712baa47-6362-4975-a62e-0018d9d2f799

Design principles selected for NY: prominent product imagery, readable naming/price, explicit size decision, visible Add to Bag action. This prototype uses a product modal; a dedicated shareable PDP route is a future step.

### Bag & checkout
- ZARA shopping bag flow: https://mobbin.com/flows/488665a5-8b6e-44f4-89b8-8e33caf6327c
- H&M purchasing an item flow: https://mobbin.com/flows/d19485c3-1be5-44b2-bd54-5b3e03818c06

Design principles selected for NY: uncluttered cart, quantity adjustment, visible subtotal, ordered steps through checkout and a clear final review. Payment entry is deliberately **not** implemented because no real payment integration has been authorized.

## Originality boundary

Mobbin screens were researched as examples of patterns and user journeys, **not copied**. No Mobbin screenshots, logos or competitor assets are bundled in this starter. The branded visual language, text and illustrations here are original or adapted from NY Atelier-owned sources.
